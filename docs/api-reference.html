POST /partner/v1/visits Submit a visit

The one endpoint a client business calls. A patient finishes your intake form and you post it here; everything after this happens on our side and is reported back to the patient. **What the payload names.** company is your account slug, visitType the treatment area (this is the category), pharmacyId the pharmacy slug you are contracted to, and masterId your own order id — which is also the idempotency key, so a retry is safe and a reused one is refused. **formObj.patientPreference[]** is what the patient chose: medId is the platform medication id (from GET /partner/v1/pharmacies/{pharmacyId}/catalog — not the pharmacy's kit code), plus strength, quantity, refills and days supply. Send patientPaidCents per line to see your own margin in the console. **Q1/A1 … Qn/An** carry the questionnaire. They are encrypted at rest and only a clinician and you can read them back. **What happens next.** The visit is checked against your entitlements, the state the patient was in, and whether that pharmacy stocks what was chosen — all refusals arrive now, as a 400 you can act on, rather than as a patient waiting. It then routes to a licensed clinician, who approves or refuses. An approval is signed, sent to the pharmacy and shipped. The patient is told at each of those four steps in their own messages. Photographs are posted separately to POST /partner/v1/visits/{visitId}/uploads.

PATCH /partner/v1/visits/{masterId} Correct a visit

Fixes the contact and delivery details on a visit you already sent — a mistyped phone number, a wrong street. Send only the fields that change. **Only until a clinician decides.** After that the address on the chart is the one they decided against and the order is with a pharmacy, so this returns 400. POST /partner/v1/visits/{masterId}/messages A patient wrote to the clinician

The other half of the DOCTOR_CHAT webhook. Your patient never sees this platform — they are on your site — so when a clinician asks them for a clearer photograph, the reply is typed somewhere we cannot reach. Post it here and it lands in the same conversation, with the clinician seeing it immediately. Send content, an image, or both. POST /partner/v1/visits/{masterId}/outcome Update a visit, or re-send its prescription

One call for two situations, because from your side it is one question — here is the corrected order — and which one it becomes depends on something you cannot see: whether a clinician has reached the visit yet. **Before a decision** the requested medications and pharmacy are replaced and you get VISIT_DATA_UPDATED. The lines you send replace the ones on the visit rather than merging with them, so send the whole order, not a patch. **After an approval** the clinician’s decision is re-issued against the corrected order and you get NEW_RX_SENT. No clinician is asked again, which is only safe because of the four guards below — the resend has to be materially the prescription that was approved: - same dosage category as the original — otherwise CATEGORY_MISMATCH - a pharmacy that stocks it — otherwise PHARMACY_MISMATCH - within 7 days of signing — otherwise TOO_LONG_AGO - within your account’s retry limit — otherwise TOO_MANY_RETRIES **After a refusal** there is no prescription to re-send, so a correction is treated as an amendment and returns VISIT_DATA_UPDATED. **After a referral** nothing is accepted: the clinician sent the patient elsewhere, and that answer was not about the medication. **No field may be null, undefined or empty.** Each is refused by name before anything is processed. apiKey must match the bearer token on the request. Every outcome is HTTP 200 with a status in the body.
